# Design sources

- [Toolsmith](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/toolsmith) by Jesse Vincent, reviewed at commit `7e45f7fb5fb3dd7ae6d84684c03205c4ad2417ee`. PA keeps the idea of a focused toolsmith, reuse before invention, and a scoped brief. This BB package does not redistribute upstream code, hooks, or its script catalog.
- [Matt Wynne's kaizen skills](https://github.com/mattwynne/skills/tree/8a6af667488e64042fa21bb1167cdbc69a3d8c6f/skills) inspired noticing friction in real use and checking that the improvement addressed it. The existing kaizen skills aren't copied or changed; they address delivery machinery, not missing PA product capabilities.
